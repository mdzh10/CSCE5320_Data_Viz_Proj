Growing up after pediatric spinal cord injury

How do independence, life satisfaction and mood differ across 45 years of follow-up for people injured before age 18, and how much of that picture is simply who is still being interviewed? Every filter below applies to every chart. Observational data: patterns are descriptive, not causal.

6 · Growing up: how many finish school, earn a degree, work and marry?

Share of interviews reaching each milestone, by approximate age at interview. Split by injury level, severity, sex or era to compare groups at the same age.

Data. NSCISC Public Use Database, 2021 annual report (Form I, Form II, record status). Cohort: 3,006 people injured before age 18. Source scores: CHART (0–100; totals of 999 = not asked), Satisfaction With Life (5–35), PHQ-2 / PHQ-9, pain interference. Only follow-up records with usable data are interviews; records flagged “lost to system” carry no scores.

Choices to know about. Severity is the ASIA grade at rehabilitation discharge (fallback: rehab admission, then acute admission); grade E and unrecorded grades are grouped as “Unknown”. Interview age and calendar year are estimated from age and year of injury plus the follow-up anniversary. SWL is rescaled from 5–35 to 0–100 only so it can share an axis; compare the shape of the lines, not their levels. Colours keep one meaning across the page: blue is physical independence, orange life satisfaction, aqua social integration; groupings (injury era, age at injury) use grey steps, and red is kept for the exception group in question 2. The “min n” slider fades small groups, it never hides them.
